# Megabonk (Manual)

## What is this?
Megabonk as a Manual game. No mod is needed: you play Megabonk normally and mark checks by hand in the Manual Client, and you hold yourself to the items you have been sent.

An optional mod, MegabonkAP, sends the checks for you and locks what you do not hold: https://github.com/bryarpatch36/MegaBonk-Archipelago

## What are the items?
- `Character - <name>`: you may only start a run with a character you hold. You start with one random character.
- `Weapon - <name>`: you may pick that weapon at a level-up. A character's own starting weapon is always allowed with that character.
- `<name> Tome`: you may pick that tome at a level-up. You start with two random tomes.
- `Progressive Forest Tier` and `Progressive Desert Tier`: the first one opens Tier 1 of that map, the second Tier 2, the third Tier 3. You start with Forest Tier 1.
- `Graveyard Access`: you may play the Graveyard.
- `Trap - Miniboss`, `Swarm`, `Elite Pack`, `Poison`, `Slow`, `Freeze` (optional, 12 in all): with the mod, it happens in your run when the item arrives. Without the mod they do nothing.
- `Bonk`: filler. It does nothing.

## What are the checks?
- With each character: complete a run, and reach Level 30.
- With each weapon: get it to Level 10 in a run.
- With each tome: get it to Level 8 in a run.
- On Forest and Desert: complete Tier 1, Tier 2 and Tier 3, and defeat each miniboss and hidden boss.
- Optional: the miniboss hunt, 15 checks for minibosses defeated in total over all your runs. It needs the mod to count them.
- On the Graveyard: collect the 4 Crypt Keys, defeat Big Bob, defeat Spooky Steve.
- Optional: each Forest and Desert challenge.

## What is the goal?
Complete the chosen tier on both Forest and Desert: `Tier 1 Bosses`, `Tier 2 Bosses` (the default) or `Tier 3 Bosses`.
